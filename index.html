The Contrarian Ship

Apps that attack what users complain about.

A 30-app challenge: ship one app every 2–3 days. Each one picks a category leader, reads its worst reviews, and removes the single loudest complaint — then gets designed like a flagship.

The road ahead

Every app owns one accent from a shared palette. The OQVIAN design system (oqvian_ui) carries the tokens, motion and widgets so each new app starts on the system, not from scratch.

The rules

One wedgeDo one thing unmistakably well. No “and also.”
The 1-star testName the incumbent and quote its worst review. If you can’t, it isn’t sharp enough.
Shared DNASame tokens, motion and type across all 30 — speed from the system, not from cutting polish.
Local-firstData on device. Cloud only when it earns its place.

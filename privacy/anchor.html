ANCHOR — Privacy

Updated 8 October 2026

ANCHOR stores worries, saved drafts, action plans, completion history and preferences on your device. It has no app account or OQVIAN data server.

Saving a plan does not schedule a notification. Calendar exports contain the worry you choose to export; importing or sharing them is your decision.

Optional voice input uses the device or browser speech recognition service. That service may process audio through its own servers and privacy terms. You can use typing instead.

Saved actions can be erased from the Saved actions screen. Exported calendar files and copies you share must be deleted separately.

The app does not include OQVIAN advertising or behavioral analytics. Fonts are bundled with the apps.

For hosted web apps, GitHub Pages handles web requests under GitHub’s privacy policy, including ordinary hosting logs. Browser storage and operating-system backups are controlled by those platforms.

Support: ifat@oqviangroup.com. Emails you send to support are handled for the purpose of responding to your request.

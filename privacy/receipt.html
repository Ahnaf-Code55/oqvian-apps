RECEIPT — Privacy

Updated 8 October 2026

RECEIPT stores kept quotes and reviewed line items on your device. There is no OQVIAN quote server or app account.

Native camera and photo selection are optional. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit on the device. Recognized images and output text are not sent to Google for processing.

Google ML Kit may contact Google for updates and sends API performance and utilization metrics. See Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms. Native store data disclosures must include this SDK behavior.

Web users paste quote text. You review amounts and categories before revealing, keeping or exporting a receipt. RECEIPT can add only the fees you supply; it cannot discover undisclosed charges.

Sharing an image or copying a summary transfers the content only when you choose to share it. Kept receipts can be erased with Clear. Copies you share remain outside the app.

The app does not include OQVIAN advertising or behavioral analytics. Fonts are bundled with the apps.

For hosted web apps, GitHub Pages handles web requests under GitHub’s privacy policy, including ordinary hosting logs. Browser storage and operating-system backups are controlled by those platforms.

Support: ifat@oqviangroup.com. Emails you send to support are handled for the purpose of responding to your request.

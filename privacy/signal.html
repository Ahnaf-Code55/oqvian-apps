SIGNAL — Privacy

Updated 8 October 2026

SIGNAL stores budgets, daily totals, saved minutes, rewards, redemption history, profile, goals, local crew entries and settings on your device.

On Android, optional Usage Access reads usage events and computes an aggregate foreground duration on your device. Package identifiers are used transiently in native memory and are not passed to Flutter, persisted or sent to OQVIAN. Device totals still need comparison against the system dashboard.

Web and iOS use manual usage entry. Crew entries are local; multi-person synchronization is not connected.

Optional native reminders are scheduled on your device after notification permission is granted. Browser reminders are unavailable.

You can export your data to the clipboard or erase it from Settings. You control any subsequent sharing of that export.

The app does not include OQVIAN advertising or behavioral analytics. Fonts are bundled with the apps.

For hosted web apps, GitHub Pages handles web requests under GitHub’s privacy policy, including ordinary hosting logs. Browser storage and operating-system backups are controlled by those platforms.

Support: ifat@oqviangroup.com. Emails you send to support are handled for the purpose of responding to your request.
